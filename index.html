01 · fintech · anti-money-laundering

Aegis

Laundering is 0.05% of the 6.9 million payments in the IBM AML dataset, and rule-based alerts mostly cry wolf. I built a pipeline with 30 behaviour and money-cycle features and a LightGBM model that ranks every payment by risk, scores live through FastAPI in about 20 ms, and comes with a Streamlit dashboard for investigators.

  • 100 / 100 top alerts are real laundering
  • 99 / 100 on a dataset it had never seen
  • 66.5% of laundering caught in the top 5,000 alerts (the old prototype: 0.6%)

Polars · LightGBM · FastAPI · Streamlit · 45 tests

02 · crypto · market intelligence

Bitcoin Market Intelligence

Fourteen years of minute-level Bitcoin prices, 7.76 million bars, turned into product, risk and operations decisions for a crypto-investing app. ETL with 47 SQL data-quality checks, a star schema, then causal inference, regime models, GARCH value-at-risk and forecasting. Every recommendation comes with its uncertainty.

Python · SQL · statsmodels · Tableau · Chart.js

03 · healthcare · matching

Hippocrates

My first version matched brain-tumour patients to 9,602 clinical trials, and only a quarter were ones they could actually join. So I rebuilt it: registered age limits, EGFR, IDH and MGMT conflicts, a site in the patient's country, and a reason for every match.

  • 24% → 100% of matches a patient could join
  • 660 valid trials the old rules had missed
  • 157 tests

Python · Tableau · pytest · synthetic patients, not medical advice
